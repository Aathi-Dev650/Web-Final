🍅 Disease Detected: Early Blight

Cause

Caused by the fungal pathogen Alternaria solani. High humidity above 70% together with temperatures between 24°C and 29°C promotes rapid fungal growth and disease spread.

Symptoms

  • Yellow spots on leaves
  • Brown circular lesions
  • Wilting of younger leaves
  • Premature leaf drop

Preventive Measures

  • Avoid overhead irrigation.
  • Maintain proper spacing for air circulation.
  • Use disease-resistant seed varieties.
  • Follow a 3-year crop rotation schedule.

Recommended Organic Remedy

  • Spray Neem Oil (5 ml/L of water).
  • Apply Trichoderma bio-fungicide.
  • Remove infected leaves immediately.
  • Spray once every 7 days until recovery.
